# Third-party rights, upstream attribution, and release controls

Zotero JournalFlow derives from [JournalLens v0.3.9](https://github.com/Lyz-623/JournalLens), as documented in README. The upstream JournalLens LICENSE reads **MIT License — Copyright (c) 2026 yunze**. The JournalFlow LICENSE preserves that original notice along with additional contributions attributed to groele. Do not replace or paraphrase the upstream copyright.

## Rights and attribution
- Upstream JournalLens: MIT; preserve the original copyright and permission notice in copies or substantial portions of its software.
- JournalFlow modifications: MIT as currently declared. Ownership in new contributions does not erase upstream copyright.
- Icons, embedded images, templates, fonts, locale files and article figures are **not automatically MIT** merely because the source code is MIT. Verify origin and rights of each item placed in a distributed XPI.
- Publisher figures/abstracts accessed at runtime remain subject to the publishers' or authors' rights and applicable API terms. Metadata retrieval does not grant a blanket license to redistribute article content.

## Checks required before the next release
- [ ] Identify exact JournalLens baseline commit/tag and record modified upstream files.
- [ ] Verify copied source retains MIT notices and attribution.
- [ ] Build an inventory of all assets and third-party source/bundled dependencies.
- [ ] Compare exact XPI files, resolved dependency licenses and notices.
- [ ] Validate publisher/API terms and any caching or export of copyrighted figures.
- [ ] Retain links to corresponding source for the shipped revision.
